TrekAssistant is operated by NeptiTech. In this document, “we,” “us” and “our” mean NeptiTech.

The short route

Your rig has a story. Here’s how we handle the information that helps you tell it.

  • Your account, your library: versions with TA Account setup link your library to your account and keep a local copy for offline use. Earlier versions may offer phone-only storage.
  • Your camera roll stays yours: camera photos, selected images and documents—not an automatic upload of your entire photo library.
  • Share the view, on your terms: choose what to include and where to send it.
  • Leaving? Two separate steps: delete your account in the app; cancel your subscription through Apple.

1. What comes along for the ride

A service receipt today. A favorite campsite tomorrow. TrekAssistant helps keep the practical details and the good memories together.

Your library can include vehicle details and mileage; maintenance and service logs; expenses, fuel entries and payment-method labels; trip destinations, dates, site numbers, gate hours and access codes; notes; photos; and documents. We handle the information you enter or select to provide these features.

When you create or use a TA Account, we process your email address, account identifier and authentication information to register you, verify access, sign you in and recover your account. Where available, you can sign in with Apple, Google or an email address and a TrekAssistant password. A display name and optional profile photo help identify your profile; the public use of that profile is explained below.

Apple or Google provides the information you authorize, which can include a provider identifier, email address and profile information. Apple may provide a private relay email address. We do not receive your Apple or Google password. Authentication credentials and session tokens are processed by our authentication service; sign-in sessions are stored using the device’s secure storage. For Sign in with Apple, we also process a protected provider credential to support revoking authorization during account deletion.

A sign-in method is a way into an account. Our authentication service can associate Apple, Google and email sign-ins that have the same verified email address with one TA Account. That account keeps the same profile and library. Apple’s private relay address or a different email address may result in a different account. We do not combine the libraries of distinct TA Accounts merely because they are used on the same phone.

For email/password access, we process verification and recovery messages to help you change or reset your TrekAssistant password. Apple and Google manage their own passwords and account recovery. The email domain alone does not determine which sign-in method you used. If you contact support, we receive the message and any contact details or attachments you choose to send.

Expenses record a payment-method label, such as “cash” or “credit card”—not your card details. TrekAssistant does not require your payment-card number or bank credentials.

Keep the memories, leave out the secrets. Please do not upload photos of credit or debit cards, passwords, PINs, security codes, bank account numbers or online-banking credentials. Before adding a receipt, photo or document, crop out or cover unnecessary sensitive details. Only add information you have permission to use. These precautions help reduce risk; they do not change our responsibilities for handling information described in this policy.

2. Your account, the cloud and offline use

Versions with TA Account setup: your vehicle and trip library is associated with the signed-in TA Account. Supported records and files are stored using Supabase, and the app keeps a local database and private files so saved information can be used offline. Offline use is not a separate anonymous account or a promise that information stays only on the phone.

Earlier versions with a phone-only choice: choosing phone-only storage does not itself upload that library to our cloud service. Purchase validation, online content, Community, private messages and support can still involve online services. An existing cloud copy is not deleted by choosing phone-only storage.

Where the app offers to bring an existing phone-only library into your account, it explains the upload and asks you to choose before linking that library. Declining that step does not itself upload the library. An already linked account library continues under its existing cloud settings.

Supported local changes are sent to the account’s cloud library automatically while the app is open and connected. Cloud updates require a connection. Pending local changes may upload when a connection returns. Conflicting versions may require your choice. Only information already saved on the device is available offline; a new sign-in or other account verification may require a connection.

When accounts change: the app opens the library associated with the signed-in TA Account. Separate accounts have separate libraries, profiles and private conversations. Account-specific cached databases and files may remain in private device storage to support returning to an account. Signing out is not a request to erase those caches or the cloud account, and it does not cancel a subscription.

Switching to phone-only storage or signing out does not delete an existing cloud copy. Use account deletion to request its removal. Local-only settings, including document pins and expiration-reminder preferences, may not appear on another device.

Currently, supported cloud attachments include photos and PDFs up to 6 MiB per file. Larger or unsupported files can remain only on the phone. A cloud copy is not a guarantee that every change has uploaded or that deleted content can be recovered.

3. Keep the memories. Choose what you share.

A photo story is yours to enjoy privately or share. The choice is always yours.

We use the system photo and document pickers for files you select. Camera access requires your permission. Selected files are copied into app storage and, when supported and linked to cloud storage, can be uploaded. Photo files may retain embedded information, including when and where they were taken.

Trip galleries and photo stories use the photos and details you choose. Sharing opens your phone’s sharing tools; you choose the destination and complete the share. TrekAssistant does not automatically publish to Instagram or Facebook. Review the photo and included details before sharing: personal information visible in the image remains visible even if a text field is excluded.

Maintenance and document-expiration reminders use local device notifications, with your permission. Notification delivery depends on your phone’s settings. These reminders do not require sending the document itself to a notification provider.

In versions offering message alerts, you can opt in to notifications for private messages and Support replies. We store a device notification token, a protected app-installation identifier, your account and sign-in session association, and limited delivery records to route alerts and handle retries. Apple Push Notification service receives the device token, generic alert text and identifiers used to open the appropriate conversation; we do not include message bodies in these notification payloads. You can turn message alerts off in the app or manage permissions in iPhone Settings. Signing out unregisters that app installation from the previous account. Notifications already delivered or submitted to Apple may remain. Delivery depends on network availability and Apple and device settings, and is not guaranteed. Delivery queue records are scheduled for cleanup after seven days; message history has the separate retention described below.

Reports and backup exports contain readable records and may include attachments. Exported backup archives are not password-encrypted by TrekAssistant. Keep them in a trusted location. Temporary export files are cleaned up by the app, but saved or shared copies remain in the destination you choose.

Park Stories: private until you publish

Community is optional. When you publish a Park Story, anyone can view the selected photos, title, post, captions, park or city and visit dates you choose to include—even without an account. Your chosen public display name, bio and profile photo identify you in Community. We do not automatically publish your account email, access codes, site number, vehicle records, receipts, documents or private trip notes.

Public text is checked against a limited English word and phrase list before publication. These checks run within our existing backend; they do not send text or photos to a separate moderation provider. They are not a complete or context-aware screening system and do not automatically inspect photo content. Published content can be reported, hidden, muted or blocked using the available Community controls. Authorized moderators can review reports, remove content and restrict abusive accounts. Earlier submissions held under the retired manual-approval workflow remain private; they are not automatically published. Their selected photos remain retained with those submissions until the associated records are removed, including through account deletion. New public submissions do not require routine admin approval.

We store Community profiles, published stories, comments, reactions, following and blocking choices, and reports to operate the feature and address abuse. Comments and your public profile are visible to other viewers; reaction totals are displayed. Reports and blocking choices are not part of a public story.

We create separate versions of published photos and re-encode them to remove original camera metadata. The picture or text itself can still reveal a person, location or private detail, so review what you select before publishing. Private drafts remain on the device where you save them. Uploads prepared for a publication that does not finish are not public and are eligible for automatic cleanup.

Unpublishing removes the story from public viewing and prevents new public photo requests. Private editable copies and an unpublished service copy may remain until account deletion or a removal request. We cannot recall screenshots, downloaded files, copies shared by other people, or provider backups. Deleting your cloud account removes its Community profile, stories and interactions; derived-file cleanup can finish afterward.

Use the in-app report and block controls, or contact info@trekassistant.com, if something should not be there.

Private messages and AI fuel reading

Private messages

Community and private messages use an online account. In earlier versions offering phone-only library storage, these online features still use an account. We store message text, sender and recipient identifiers, delivery order and times, read status, messaging preferences, and deletion or hiding choices in Supabase to operate the service. Messages are visible to the participants, rather than the public feed. They are not end-to-end encrypted.

Deleting a message or conversation from your inbox removes it from your view; it does not recall the message or remove the other participant’s copy. A recipient can report a received message. Authorized moderators can review that reported message and its report details; the review tool does not reveal the rest of the private conversation. Service operators and providers may process data as needed for operation, security, support or legal obligations. Do not send information that you do not want the recipient to keep.

Messages to Support

Where in-app support is available, your signed-in account can send a private message directly to TrekAssistant support without entering a recipient’s username or email. Replies appear as Admin. This is support correspondence, separate from person-to-person Community messages and the public feed.

We store support message text, your account identifier, sender identifiers, timestamps, conversation and read-status information in Supabase. Authorized support staff can see the conversation together with your account email and display name to identify and answer your request. Other travelers cannot access it. These messages are not end-to-end encrypted, and the service does not promise an immediate reply. Do not send passwords, verification codes or unnecessary sensitive information.

AI fuel reading

When you choose to scan a pump display or fuel receipt, the selected photo is sent through our backend to OpenAI to identify fuel values and available receipt details. The scan requires an online connection, including in versions that offer phone-only library storage. Our backend removes image EXIF metadata before sending the scan and requests that OpenAI not store the response for later retrieval. This is not a promise of zero retention: OpenAI may retain data for abuse monitoring and other applicable exceptions under its data controls.

Our scan service processes the photo and returns the readings without saving the photo or extracted text in the scan service database. It retains account-linked usage counts and request/status records to manage service access and costs. If you save the expense or attach the receipt, that record or photo follows the library-storage behavior available in your app version, including account-linked cloud updates where enabled. Check and correct AI readings before saving. Manual entry remains available.

5. When it’s time to leave

We keep cloud account information and your linked library while needed to provide the service, unless you delete the records or request account deletion. In-app support conversations are kept to answer requests and provide conversation history while the account exists; they are removed from the active support database when the associated account is deleted. Separately delivered support emails and other necessary business records may be kept as needed to resolve requests or meet legal obligations. Service providers can retain separate logs, backups, purchase records and records required by law.

Once your deletion request is accepted, we take care of the processing.

Use Delete account in the app to request removal of your cloud account, linked cloud records and uploaded files. We verify the request before accepting it. Once accepted, removal runs automatically; you do not need to leave the app open. The process also requests deletion of the linked RevenueCat customer profile.

In the TA Account flow: deletion includes the account’s cloud library and its local records and supported files on the phone where you complete the flow. Account deletion also removes the associated Community profile and content and in-app support conversation from active service records. Earlier versions may offer a separate choice to keep phone-only records; where that choice is offered, the confirmation explains what remains.

After you confirm deletion, the app may restrict access while the request is verified, processed and local cleanup is completed. You can close the app and return to check progress. Server processing does not require the app to remain open; cleanup of this phone completes when the app can run. A progress screen is not a promise that every provider copy has already been erased. An error or interruption may require a retry. Deleting the cloud account does not remotely erase a device that is offline. Local copies and device backups may remain on other devices and may need to be removed separately.

Retention depends on the record’s purpose. Account and library records remain while you use the service, subject to your deletion choices. In-app support history remains with the account until deleted with it. Separately delivered support email may remain while needed to answer the request, resolve a dispute or satisfy a legal obligation. Limited deletion and security records can remain to complete removal, handle retries and prevent removed accounts from writing again; those records do not provide a usable copy of your deleted vehicle library. For a request about retained information, contact info@trekassistant.com.

Authentication email delivery is a separate service. Resend currently describes 30-day email and log retention for its standard Free, Pro and Scale plans; this is not a deadline for deleting a message already delivered to your mailbox. Enterprise arrangements and legally required exceptions can differ. Resend retention information. Supabase backup retention depends on the subscribed plan, and RevenueCat and email providers have separate operational, security, purchase and backup records. We do not promise that deleting a TA Account immediately erases every provider copy.

We retain limited request, status and account-blocking information needed to complete deletion, handle failures and prevent old account sessions from writing again. Account deletion does not promise immediate erasure of every provider backup, operational log or independent Apple purchase record. Their retention varies by service and record type.

Deletion does not remove previously delivered emails, exported backups or copies shared elsewhere. Restoring an Apple purchase or using the app again can create a new purchase-service profile; it does not restore your deleted cloud library.

Deleting the app is not the same as deleting your cloud account. Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription. Device backups and app offloading may also preserve local copies.

6. You’re in the driver’s seat

You can edit or delete records, manage device permissions, export your library and request account deletion. Storage choices depend on your app version. Keep important originals: losing or deleting the app can mean losing phone-only records. Apple device-transfer and backup behavior depends on your device and settings; TrekAssistant does not guarantee a complete automatic restore.

For help accessing, correcting or deleting information, or exercising privacy rights available under the law where you live, email info@trekassistant.com. We may need to verify that the request belongs to you. Do not send your password or verification codes.
