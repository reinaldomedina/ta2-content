This requirement does not remove our legal responsibilities for handling uploaded information or your rights under applicable law.

You control which supported content to share and the destination. Check the preview for personal details, access codes or information about other people before sharing.

Photo and file limits may apply. The current trip gallery supports up to 30 photos per trip and up to three photos per Explore category. Current supported cloud files are limited to 6 MiB each. Review what is stored locally before changing or replacing a photo.

A little kindness goes a long way

Limited deletion-status information, provider backups, logs and independent purchase records may remain as explained in our Privacy Policy. A later purchase restoration can create a new purchase-service profile without restoring the deleted library. Cancel any unwanted Apple subscription separately.
